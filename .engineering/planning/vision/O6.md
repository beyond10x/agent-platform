# Vision: Self-improvement with evidence

## Outcome

Agent revisions, tasks, attempts, tool decisions, usage, outcomes, and feedback form a durable evidence chain from one version to the next.

## Exit Evidence

A proposed revision names the prior run evidence that motivated it, evaluations compare immutable revisions, and activation remains an attributable reversible decision rather than a silent rewrite.
